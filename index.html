Monday Coffee Expansion: SQL Market Analysis

Which three cities in India should a coffee brand pick for its first physical stores? Answered end to end in SQL.

Objective. Use historical sales data to move the brand from online to physical retail. Evaluate consumer demand, market penetration, and regional sales trends, then identify the three best cities for a launch.
Approach. Joined sales, customers, product, and city tables; estimated the coffee-consuming population per city (25% of population, per the brief); ranked products by city; tracked month-over-month growth; and compared average sale per customer against rent per customer.
Result. A ranked recommendation: Pune, Delhi, and Jaipur, each backed by revenue, customer, market-size, and rent metrics.
